---
description: Enforce plan-before-execute discipline on every request.
trigger: always
---

Before writing ANY code, silently assess: "Do I fully understand what's asked? What's the simplest way to test this? What could go wrong?" Write a one‑line plan into the scratchpad. After executing, verify with a test. This prevents the "rushing into the wrong solution" anti‑pattern.
